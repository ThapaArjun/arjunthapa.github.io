# Research

## Red Panda Ecology and Conservation
I investigate red panda distribution, habitat requirements,
genetic diversity and conservation threats across the Himalayas.

## Climate Change and Biodiversity
I study how climate and land-cover changes affect threatened
wildlife, their distributions and potential climate refugia.

## Habitat Connectivity
My research examines wildlife corridors, habitat occupancy
and landscape changes.

## Human–Wildlife Coexistence
I investigate human–wildlife conflict and approaches that
support wildlife conservation and local livelihoods.
