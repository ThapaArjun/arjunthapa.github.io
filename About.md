# About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Wildlife Conservation at the Institute of Forestry, Hetauda Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal. My work focuses on understanding and conserving threatened mammals, with a particular interest in red pandas and Himalayan biodiversity.

I completed my PhD in Ecology at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, conducting research at the Institute of Zoology on red panda conservation ecology and genetics across the species’ geographic range. I subsequently undertook postdoctoral research at the Institute of Zoology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, through the President’s International Fellowship Initiative.

My research connects ecological fieldwork with species distribution modelling, spatial analysis and genetic approaches. Alongside research, I teach wildlife conservation, supervise students and contribute to conservation planning and community-based wildlife monitoring.
